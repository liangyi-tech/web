Liangyi (Chengdu) Technology Co., Ltd. is a Chengdu company that makes software for personal improvement. The company measures the days and habits in front of a person, and leaves the change with them. Daymark (刻度) is one product now in progress: habits, countdowns, and days of waiting, kept on the device.

刻度

Daymark

一次只看一件事。习惯、倒数日和等待的日子记在一起。天数按累计计算，中间停过，已经记下的仍然算数。每件记录旁边有一个随天数长大的伙伴，伙伴和已记下的天数都会留着。内容留在使用者的设备上。

刻度是量译正在做的一件产品。
